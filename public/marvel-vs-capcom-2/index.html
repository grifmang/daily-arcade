Chains & air attacks

Directions are shown facing right; reverse left and right when facing left. P means either punch; K means either kick. Press both listed buttons together for a hyper.

On contact, repeat a light button for its medium attack, then chain onward using the character's allowed sequence. Crouch and jump change the attacks. Ryu's forward + HP / HK are command normals. Hold Cyclops's standing HK for its follow-up.
